# WAYPOINT — read first

## What is ready

A runnable Draft of a premium outdoor store: homepage, searchable/filterable 24-item demo catalog, product pages, favorites, Bag, route-kit builder, checkout UX, account shell and Journal.

## Strategy

WAYPOINT sells a curated route-ready kit, not a marketplace. The signature feature is **Route assembly**: visitors input five travel conditions and receive a transparent starter kit.

## What is only a hypothesis

Products, prices, ratings, availability, technical specifications, delivery, payment, account and editorial material are demo content. Images are original Draft visuals, not proof of real products or tests.

## What the owner must supply before production

Approved product/PIM data, legal seller details, returns/delivery/payment policy, real assets, integrations, CRM/order system, consent/tracking setup and production domain.
