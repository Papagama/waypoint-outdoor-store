# Business and UX strategy

## Goal

Help a visitor build a concise, compatible outdoor kit and move to checkout without browsing an uncurated marketplace.

## Primary scenarios

1. Discover brand → explore categories → open a product → add it to Bag.
2. Start from a trip need → answer five route questions → add a generated kit.
3. Search a product/category → filter the catalog → compare cards → save or buy later.
4. Return to Bag → adjust quantity → move through contact, delivery and payment screens.

## Sitemap

`/`, `/catalog`, `/catalog/[category]`, `/product/[slug]`, `/kit`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, `/account`, `/journal`, `/journal/[slug]`.

## Conversion approach

- Primary CTA: catalog or kit builder, depending on the visitor’s confidence.
- Trust: state integration limits plainly instead of fabricating delivery claims, reviews or payment availability.
- Re-entry: saved gear and Journal are prepared as repeat-visit surfaces.
