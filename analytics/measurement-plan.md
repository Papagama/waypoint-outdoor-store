# Measurement plan

Proposed events after analytics consent is implemented: `view_catalog`, `apply_filter`, `search`, `view_product`, `save_product`, `add_to_bag`, `start_kit`, `complete_kit`, `start_checkout`, `checkout_validation_error`, `purchase`.

Core hypotheses: kit builder improves first-visit add-to-bag rate; category-first catalog reduces empty-search exits; transparent product information reduces checkout abandonment. No measurement is active in Draft.
