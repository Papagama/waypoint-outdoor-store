# Design guardrails

- Route markers explain context; they never replace product information.
- Orange indicates a decision, state or brand signal, not decoration.
- Catalog cards stay comparable and accessible; editorial asymmetry is for storytelling sections only.
- Do not add glass, gradients, rounded-card bento grids, fake dashboard graphics, neon or copied reference layouts.
- Keep high-contrast text on an opaque or image-darkened surface.
