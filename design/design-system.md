# Design system

- **Core colours:** stone `#F1F0EB`, ink `#171816`, moss `#59644B`, safety orange `#D45226`.
- **Typography:** neutral system grotesk; display scale relies on tight tracking and strong size contrast. Serif italics are reserved for a route/emotion cue.
- **Spacing:** 10–16px inner rhythm; 64–140px section rhythm.
- **Components:** wordmark, route label, primary/secondary button, product card, filter fieldset, quantity control, drawer, bottom sheet, accordion.
- **Responsive rule:** at ≤720px catalog becomes two-up, filter becomes a sheet, product actions remain thumb-reachable, and hero copy has a protected dark layer.
- **Motion:** 180–500ms image/interaction transitions only; all motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
